Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Back9 Trades LLC ("Back9," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit back9trades.com, use the Back9 Trades client portal (the "Portal"), request or receive our repair and maintenance services, or communicate with us. It applies to our clients and their representatives, and to residents and occupants of the properties where we work.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact and account information. Name, company, title, email, phone number, billing address, and Portal login details.
  • Property and work information. Property addresses, unit numbers, access instructions, work requests, quotes, job notes, and service history.
  • Resident information. When a client asks us to coordinate access, we receive resident names, unit numbers, and phone numbers or email addresses. We use this only to schedule and complete the work.
  • Photos and job records. Before, during, and after photos, and completion records. Photos may incidentally show the interior of a unit or personal belongings.
  • Payment information. Card and bank details you enter in the Portal are collected and processed by our payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details such as card type and last four digits. We do not store full card or bank account numbers.
  • Communications. Emails, text messages, calls, and messages sent through the Portal or our website forms.
  • Technical information. IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected through server logs and cookies needed to operate the website and Portal.

2. How We Use Information

  • To receive, schedule, dispatch, perform, and document the services you request.
  • To send quotes, invoices, receipts, appointment reminders, and service updates.
  • To process payments and manage accounts.
  • To respond to questions, warranty claims, and support requests.
  • To maintain business, tax, insurance, and permit records.
  • To secure our systems, prevent fraud, and comply with law.
  • To improve our services and operations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as follows:

  • With our clients. Job records, photos, and resident scheduling details are shared with the property owner or manager who engaged us for that property.
  • With service providers. Vendors who help us operate, including our field service and Portal software provider, payment processors, email and text messaging providers, website hosting, accounting, and payroll. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • With subcontractors. Licensed trade partners who perform part of a job receive the information needed to do that work.
  • For legal reasons. To comply with law, a subpoena, or a permit or code requirement, to enforce our agreements, or to protect the rights and safety of any person.
  • In a business transfer. In connection with a merger, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to this Policy.

4. Text Messages

If you provide a mobile number, we may send text messages about appointments, arrivals, quotes, invoices, and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

5. Cookies

Our website uses only the cookies and logs needed to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and measure basic performance. The Portal is hosted by our field service software provider and uses cookies needed for login and security. We do not use advertising cookies on our website. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though the Portal may not work without them.

6. Data Retention

We keep job records, photos, invoices, and related communications for as long as needed to provide services, honor warranties, and meet tax, insurance, licensing, and legal requirements, generally seven years after the job is completed. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections, access controls, and vendors with industry-standard security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please keep your Portal credentials confidential and tell us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

8. Your Choices and California Rights

You may update your contact information in the Portal or by contacting us. You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP and out of non-essential emails by using the unsubscribe link. Service and billing messages will continue while you have an active account.

California residents may, to the extent provided by applicable law, request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, and may request information about how it is shared. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and we may retain information we are required to keep by law. Residents of properties we service may also direct requests to the property owner or manager, who controls the service relationship.

9. Children

Our website and Portal are intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 through the website or Portal.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy by posting a new version on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the Portal or by email where appropriate.
